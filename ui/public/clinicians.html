Medical AI is only as safe as the clinicians checking it.

Health-AI companies build tools that respond to patients directly. They need licensed clinicians to review, correct, and safety-check what the AI produces. Senebiclabs connects those companies with an expert clinician panel — and we'd like you on it.

What you'd do

Apply your clinical judgment to make medical AI safer and more accurate. Depending on the project:

  • Check and correct the AI's answers to clinical questions
  • Judge how it handles risk — whether it responds safely to high-risk situations
  • Compare two responses and rank which is clinically stronger
  • Write the gold-standard answer yourself

We match the work to your specialty and interests.

Who we're looking for

Licensed clinicians across specialties. Our current projects focus on mental health — psychiatrists, clinical psychologists, psychiatric nurses, and counsellors — with more specialties to come.
